How we build

Rules of the garage

An identity you control

The On-Call app does everything as the administrator who signed in. Posture runs as that administrator, or on a schedule as an identity you create in your own Azure. Your Conditional Access, your role assignments and your audit log apply, and you can revoke any of it. The one exception is opt-in: Instant alerts reads your logs as an app with read-only permissions that your organization approves and can remove.

Reading is kept apart from changing

Posture never writes to a tenant. On-Call signs in through an app that can only read, and each kind of change goes through its own app that your organization approves separately, or never approves at all.

Your data stays yours

Posture's evidence and reports stay in your folder or your storage account. On-Call's passwords and keys travel from Microsoft to your device and are never written to disk, with no Kainor server in that path. The one hosted service, Instant alerts, is off until an organization opts in.

Read it before you trust it

The source of both products is published, every permission is listed with its reason, and nothing phones home with analytics or telemetry.
