TenantKeep On-Call

TenantKeep On-Call signs in through a Microsoft app that can only read. Anything that changes your tenant goes through a separate app, and each one works only after an administrator approves it here. Approving does not give anyone new rights: a person can still do only what their own Microsoft roles allow, and every action is in your audit log under their name.

Who can approve: a Global Administrator, or another role your organization allows to grant consent for the whole organization. Each button opens Microsoft's own approval screen, which lists the same permissions before you accept. To see or remove an approval later, find the app by name under Enterprise applications in the Entra admin center.

TenantKeep Posture

What you consented to

TenantKeep Posture is a multi-tenant application published by Kainor LLC with delegated, read-only permissions and no credentials of its own. Every token it ever holds belongs to the administrator who signed in and goes no further than that administrator's roles. The full list of permissions is in the module's app.json and was on the consent screen. Remove the consent at any time by deleting the enterprise application in Entra ID; the next run says consent is needed.
