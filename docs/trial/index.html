TenantKeep Posture

Free trial

Every domain, one tenant, thirty days.

Thirty days of every domain in the report, for one tenant, free. The Conditional Access domain needs no licence at all.

What you send, and why

  • Tenant id: the licence is bound to it. Start-PostureRun prints this page's link with it filled in.
  • Work email: the licence arrives there. Its domain must be one your tenant has verified, which is how we know the request comes from inside your organisation.
  • Organisation: the name the report prints on its cover.

That is all we keep: one row recording that this tenant had its trial. Nothing about your tenant is collected, now or when you run the module; verification of the licence is offline.

When it arrives

Save the attachment, then run Initialize-PostureClient -LicensePath <file> once and Start-PostureRun as usual. One trial per tenant; reply to the email if you need longer.
