Why you can let it in

Built for people who are paid to be suspicious

Your sign-in, not ours

Delegated Microsoft authentication only. The app can do only what your account can already do, and every action lands in your own tenant's audit log.

A read-only sign-in

The app signs in through an app registration that holds read permissions only. Anything that changes your tenant goes through a separate registration that your organization approves separately, or never approves at all.

No vendor server in the credential path

Passwords and keys travel from Microsoft Graph to your device and are never written to disk. No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no account to create.

Verifiable

The source is published, and every permission is listed with its reason. Retrieve a password and watch the read appear in your own audit log.

What it does

The work that cannot wait for a laptop

LAPSlock

Windows LAPS local administrator passwords, earlier passwords, and BitLocker recovery keys from Entra ID and Intune. Face ID before every reveal, a 60-second window, and a clipboard that clears itself. Experimental: a Mac's admin password, for Macs enrolled through Automated Device Enrollment.

Privileged Access

Activate an eligible Entra role or PIM for Groups membership under your organization's own policy, and approve or deny the requests waiting for you.

People

Find a person, see whether they can sign in, and read why each sign-in failed, with the Conditional Access policy that blocked it named. Revoke sessions, block sign-in, remove sign-in methods, issue a Temporary Access Pass, or run a leaver checklist.

Tenant Pulse

Service health, Message Center posts that need action, secrets and certificates about to expire, the Apple push certificate, and Secure Score.

Conditional Access

Every policy, read-only, newest change first, with assignments resolved to names and who changed it.

Security

Open Defender incidents with their alerts, recommended actions and everyone involved, and fixed, read-only email investigation queries.

Threat Digest

The week's real-world threats, ranked for a Microsoft shop from CISA and Microsoft sources.

Device Actions

Sync, restart, remote lock, locate, key rotation, retire and wipe, each confirmed before it is sent and written to an exportable shift log.

Alerts

Notifications for the things worth waking for, and optional Instant alerts for break-glass sign-ins and Conditional Access changes.

Apple Watch

Your active roles and pending approvals on your wrist.

Organizations

Switch between customer tenants, run a saved search across all of them, and always see which one you are in.

Actions

Every change is gated

Off until you turn it on

Each kind of action has its own switch and its own Microsoft approval. An organization that wants a read-only tool approves nothing more and has one.

Checked before it is sent

Your role is checked first, destructive actions need the name typed, and there is a ten-second window to undo during which nothing has left the phone.

Managed by Intune if you want

An app configuration policy can turn modules off, set the confirmation level, and pin the app to your tenant.

Getting started

Free to verify, then priced for real work

Free includes the device list, five credential reveals every 30 days, Tenant Pulse, Conditional Access and the Threat Digest. Pro removes the reveal limit and adds People, Security incidents, email investigation, saved searches and Instant alerts. The MSP plan adds switching between customer organizations.

Step one, for an administrator

Approve the read-only app

One approval for the whole organization lets your people sign in. It grants read permissions only. Approve an action app later, or never.
